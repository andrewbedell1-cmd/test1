About

Where Strategy
Meets Craft

Laura Delahunt-Devlin is a globally recognised beauty executive turned independent consultant, bringing over 15 years of prestige and mass-market expertise to emerging and established brands alike.

With a deep understanding of the full product lifecycle — from concept and formulation briefing through to global launch strategy — Laura partners with founders, brand teams, and investors to build products that resonate, perform, and endure.

Her career spans iconic houses including Tom Ford Beauty, NARS Cosmetics, Smashbox (The Estée Lauder Companies), and Maybelline New York (L'Oréal), where she held senior global roles across complexion, skincare, fragrance, lip, and eye.

Educated at McGill University (BCom, Marketing & Entrepreneurship, with Honors), Laura combines rigorous commercial thinking with a genuine passion for beauty innovation.
